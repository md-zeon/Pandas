# EDA (Exploratory Data Analysis)

1. **Understanding the Data**: Begin by examining the dataset to understand its structure, types of variables, and overall characteristics. This includes checking the number of rows and columns, data types, and basic statistics.
2. **Data Cleaning**: Identify and handle missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies in the data. This step ensures that the dataset is accurate and reliable for analysis.
3. **Data Visualization**: Use visualizations such as histograms, box plots, scatter plots, and bar charts to explore the distribution of variables, relationships between features, and potential outliers.
4. **Statistical Analysis**: Perform statistical tests and calculations to summarize the data, identify trends, and detect patterns. This may include measures of central tendency, variability, correlation analysis, and hypothesis testing.
5. **Feature Engineering**: Create new features or transform existing ones to enhance the dataset's predictive power. This may involve encoding categorical variables, scaling numerical features, or generating interaction terms.
